🌙 Cycle phases

🩸 Menstrual

The period phase. Energy and experiences vary from person to person.

🌱 Follicular

A phase after the period where hormone patterns change as the body prepares for ovulation.

🌕 Ovulatory

Ovulation is the release of an egg. Timing can vary considerably.

🍂 Luteal

The phase after ovulation and before the next period.

Why did Cycura suggest this?

The real Cycura architecture includes an explainability layer so users can understand why an estimate or recommendation was generated.

Example:

“This activity appeared because you selected art as an interest and your timetable showed a free 30-minute window.”

⚠️ Prototype notice

Cycura is a student project prototype. Cycle estimates are approximate and health information is educational. A production health application would require medical review, security testing, privacy compliance, validated models and professional oversight.
